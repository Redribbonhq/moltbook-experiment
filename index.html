About
The Moltbook Experiment is a public live study of binary randomness under conditions of collective observation. A new 0 or 1 is generated at fixed intervals. Outputs are logged, summarized, and displayed in real time.
Interpretation is left to the observer. The system is designed for transparency and simple auditable mechanics.
